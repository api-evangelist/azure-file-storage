As Azure continues to evolve to support modern workload patterns, we are refining how transactions are measured for Standard HDD Managed Disks . This update helps align billing more closely with actual I/O behavior while maintaining predictable cost controls for customers. In this post, we’ll walk through what’s changing, how transaction billing is calculated, and what this may mean for your workloads.
