Organizations continue to look for practical ways to manage growing volumes of unstructured data while maintaining the performance, resiliency, and familiar filesystem capabilities their applications require. Customers with existing PowerScale environments also want options for extending those investments to Azure without taking on the work of deploying and operating the underlying infrastructure. Today, we are pleased to announce the general availability of Dell Managed PowerScale for Microsoft Azure.
