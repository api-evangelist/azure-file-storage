We are excited to announce the General Availability of client-side data integrity in Azure Blob Storage. Blob Storage has long supported integrity validation, starting with MD5 as part of the original HTTP standards and adding CRC64-NVME support in 2019. Today, CRC64-NVME is now integrated into the latest Azure Blob SDKs for .NET, C++, and JavaScript, extending Azure’s data integrity protections all the way into client applications.
